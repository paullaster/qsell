# Reports benchmark study (R-0a), first pass

**Status:** first pass, 2026-11-22. Part of [`reports-module.md`](reports-module.md) section 20.3.
**What this is, honestly:** a study of what each system **publicly documents** about reporting (vendor help pages, product pages, a few partner and third-party summaries), taken on 2026-11-22. **It is not a hands-on trial.** No system was installed or used, and every row says where the claim came from and how sure it is. Anything marked *to trial* must be confirmed by using the product before it drives a decision. Plan tiers and report lists change; do not quote this document as fact about a competitor.

## 1. Method

1. A **scorecard** (section 4) of 150 questions in 12 areas, each answerable "yes, how well, how many clicks".
2. A **documentation pass** (this document): one search per system, reading the official help or product pages where the search reached them, recording only what the sources say.
3. A **hands-on pass** (to do): trial accounts or demos of the systems marked *to trial*, scoring the full scorecard with a real year of seeded data, timing each answer and screenshotting phone views.
4. The result decides the build order of the reports module and what we deliberately do better.

## 3. What this changes in the design

Items already in `reports-module.md` that the study **supports**: dimension-based analysis views, financial layouts over the chart of accounts, role dashboards with KPIs, saved searches with alerts, pivots, scheduled email, per-action permissions, stock turnover, GMROI, sell-through, dead stock, team performance, tax summaries, aging reports, budget reports and cash flow forecast.

New requirements or sharper rules found, now added to the design (see the edits at the end of `reports-module.md`):

1. **Return readiness view for VAT** (from Tally): for any tax period, every document is shown as included, needing correction (with the reason: missing PIN, unsigned, wrong rate), or excluded (with the reason), each drillable, so the return is prepared with no surprises.
2. **Keyboard drill on every line** of every table and statement, down to the source document, with a detail toggle (from Tally).
3. **Exception and activity reports** (from Square): voids, refunds, comps, discounts, price overrides, stock adjustments and logins by person, branch and hour, with thresholds and alerts. Already partly in section 6.3; now a named family.
4. **Retail performance measures** (from Lightspeed), all included: customer capture rate (share of sales with a known customer), upsell percentage (share of baskets with more than one item or an added item), sales by hour of day, margin alert, sell-through, GMROI. These need rules in the measure catalogue.
5. **Sales and supply on a map** (from Yonyou's map views): sales, margin and deliveries by town, zone and branch catchment, using the existing zones, branch pins and delivery pins.
6. **Inventory ageing with configurable bands** (from SAP's gap and Zoho): the bands are company data, and ageing is by receipt date through the stock ledger.
7. **Document-state rule in every definition** (from Zoho): each measure states which states count (for example drafts, held orders, cancelled and failed orders are excluded) and this is part of the printed definition.
8. **No feature tiers inside reports:** custom reports, favourites, goals, schedules and exports are included for every customer (Lightspeed gates them by plan). Permissions govern access, not price.
9. **Time-limited access:** a permission can carry a look-back limit (Square's 90 days), useful for branch staff who should see this week but not last year.
10. **Local statutory forms as a checklist to verify:** the Kenya payroll documents seen in Sage's notes (PAYE return, P9A and P9B certificates, fringe benefit, housing levy, other levy reports) go on the Kenya pack checklist, each to be confirmed against the authority's current form before it is built.

## 4. The scorecard (150 questions)

Scoring per question per system: 0 no, 1 partly, 2 yes, 3 best in class; plus **clicks to answer**, **time on a year of data**, **phone view works (y/n)**, **ties to the books (y/n)**, **who may see it**. Questions are written as the owner or officer would ask them.

**A. Executive (12)**
A1 How are we doing today, this week, this month against last year? A2 Which branch is best and worst and why? A3 What is our cash position now and in 13 weeks? A4 What needs my attention today? A5 Are we on budget this month and for the year? A6 Which targets are we missing and what run rate fixes it? A7 What changed in profit versus last month and why (price, volume, mix)? A8 One-page monthly pack for the board? A9 Which products, customers or branches make 80% of profit? A10 What is our stock worth and how fast does it turn? A11 What do customers owe and what do we owe? A12 Can I see all this on my phone in under five seconds?

**B. Sales (18)**

B10 Basket size, items per basket, attach rate. B11 Discount and promotion cost and uplift. B12 Price variance by branch and customer. B13 Returns by product, reason, branch. ## 5. Decision: the hands-on pass is skipped (2026-11-22)

The owner decided to skip the trial pass. R-0a is therefore closed with the documentation study above as its basis. Consequences, stated plainly:

- The build order follows this study, the report catalogue in `reports-module.md` and the owner's list of first reports, not a measured comparison.
- Every statement about a competitor stays *unconfirmed* and must not appear in sales material or documentation as fact. The platform's own claims are made only about what it does.
- The scorecard (section 4) remains in force as the **acceptance checklist for our own reports**: each report, when built, is scored against its questions, so quality is still measured, only against our own bar.
- The pass can be reopened at any time (for example if a first client names a competitor they are replacing); nothing else depends on it.

### Original plan for the hands-on pass (not being run)

1. Obtain trials or demos, in this order: Business Central, Odoo, Zoho Books and Inventory, Lightspeed or Square, NetSuite (demo), TallyPrime, Sage Kenya payroll, and any Kenyan retail or distribution ERP and POS the owner names. Record the products your first clients already use or have rejected.
2. Seed each trial with a comparable year of data (the report demo seed planned in R-0).
3. Score the 150 questions, time them, capture phone screenshots.
4. Write the result as a matrix in this folder, then confirm or reverse the rows marked in section 2.
5. Re-order slices R-2 to R-4 by the largest gaps in what a first client would ask for in the first week.

## 6. What we will not claim from this study

That a competitor lacks a feature merely because a search did not show it; that any figure about a competitor's report counts or plans is current; or that we have matched a product we have not used. The only statements that stand without a trial are the ones in the **Source quality A** rows, as of the date above.
